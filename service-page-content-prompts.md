```
professional, benefit-focused, short sentences, one-time-service framing
(not a subscription). Do not invent specific customer names, exact issue
counts, or claims that can't be backed up — keep stats generic and round.

Output these sections, in this order:

1. Page title: "WordPress [SERVICE NAME] Service" (or a short punchy variant)
2. One-sentence subtitle describing the problem this solves
3. A stats/trust line in exactly this format:
   "[X]+ [relevant metric]. 200+ five-star reviews. [Service] from $[PRICE]. Money-back guarantee."
4. CTA button label: "Start My [Service]"
5. Four short trust badges: After-Sales Support / Experienced / Fair Pricing / Fast
6. A section titled "Need a One-Time [Service]?" — 2 short paragraphs on why
   someone needs this and what problem it solves
7. A "How Our [Service] Works" section with exactly 3 numbered steps, each
   with a 2-4 word title and a one-sentence description
8. An "About Our [Service] Service" section with 4-6 feature cards, each
   with a short title (2-4 words) and a 1-2 sentence description of what's
   actually included
9. A one-to-two sentence "Trusted [Service] Service" trust statement
10. An FAQ section with 4-8 question/answer pairs covering pricing,
    timeline, what access is needed, and common concerns
11. A closing CTA section: a heading, one reinforcing paragraph, and the
    same CTA button from step 4

Write for: [SERVICE NAME] — [one-line description of what it covers]
Starting price: $[PRICE]
Must cover these sub-features: [bullet list]
```
